EmbeddedShop now targets the embedded and robotics domain instead of a generic lifestyle storefront.

Visual direction:
- Control-room inspired UI
- Graphite and deep navy backgrounds
- Cyan and lime accents for active states
- Panel-based cards with subtle technical framing
- Product visuals rendered as hardware modules and chip panels

Catalog direction:
- Raspberry Pi and edge compute boards
- FPGA development boards
- Robot chassis and motion-control hardware
- Lidar, stereo vision, and sensing modules
- Power, field IO, and industrial connectivity boards

Core flows:
- Home landing with curated hardware collections
- Catalog search with category and stock filters
- Product detail with specs, compatibility, and quick actions
- Saved items, cart, and procurement-oriented checkout
- Profile preferences and order history
